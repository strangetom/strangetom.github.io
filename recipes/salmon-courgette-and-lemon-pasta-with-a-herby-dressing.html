Salmon, courgette and lemon pasta with a herby dressing

Serves 2    |     Prep 6 mins     |     Cook 10 mins

This fresh and zingy creamy dressing, bursting with fresh herbs, yoghurt and plenty of black pepper, pairs perfectly with roasted salmon and charred courgettes. While fresh salmon is used here, tinned or smoked salmon are also great budget-friendly options.

Source: The Ultimate Air Fryer Cookbook

Ingredients

    /    
    /    
    /    
    /    
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Zest and juice of 2 lemons
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper, plus extra to serve
  • 2 courgettes, thinly sliced into 5 mm rounds on an angle
  • 400 g fresh fusilli pasta
  • 300 g skinless salmon fillets
  • 200 g Greek yoghurt
  • 30 g mixed fresh herbs, parsley, basil, dill, roughly chopped, plus extra to serve
