ThaiGreen Thai salmon parcels

Serves 2    |     Prep 10 mins     |     Cook 12 mins

What I love most about this recipe is how versatile it is; it’s such a great meal to use up any leftover veg, and trust me when I say it never gets boring! I’m always chopping and changing the vegetables based on what’s in my fridge. Mix it up with sliced peppers, pak choi, green beans, mushrooms or shredded cabbage. This recipe is bursting with vibrant flavours, tender salmon and crunchy veggies, all wrapped up and cooked to perfection in your air fryer.

Source: The Ultimate Air Fryer Cookbook
