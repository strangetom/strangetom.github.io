Potato rösti with smoked ham and eggs

Serves 4    |     Prep 10 mins     |     Cook 38 mins

Just a handful of humble ingredients come together here for a joyous lunch (or even breakfast) that’s far easier to make in a roasting pan than in a frying pan. As ever, keep your eyes glued to the eggs as they bake: all ovens are different and you want the yolks to spill their loveliness over the rösti when you cut into them, not require a carving knife.

Source: Foolproof Roasting Pan: 60 Effortless One-Pan Recipes Packed with Flavour

Ingredients

    /    
    /

    /    
  • 3 tbsp duck or goose fat, or olive oil
  • 4 medium waxy potatoes (about 180 g each)
  • 4 thick slices smoked ham, chopped (about 40 g each)
  • sea salt flakes and freshly ground black pepper
  • 4 large eggs
