VietnameseBò Kho-style steak and lemongrass pie

Serves 6    |     Prep 20 mins     |     Cook 3 hr

I recently went through a stage of making everything into a pie, as I discovered pre-made pastry at my local supermarket. This particular recipe is like a Vietnamese Bò Kho – a traditional, rich and comforting beef stew – meets a classic steak pie, and it’s a match made in heaven! I hope you enjoy it as much as I do.

Source: Vietnamese Made Easy
