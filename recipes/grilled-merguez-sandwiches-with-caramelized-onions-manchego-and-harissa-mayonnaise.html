Grilled merguez sandwiches with caramelized onions, manchego, and harissa mayonnaise

Serves 4    |     Prep 10 mins     |     Cook 15 mins

Grilled merguez on a toasted baguette with sweet caramelized onion, sharp Manchego cheese, fresh cilantro, and spicy harissa mayo makes for one insanely awesome sandwich.

Source: www.seriouseats.com

Ingredients

    /    
    /    
    /    
    /    
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • 8 links merguez
  • 1 French baguette
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ cup caramelized onions
  • ⅓ lb manchego cheese, grated
  • 1 bunch fresh cilantro
