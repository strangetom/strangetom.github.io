Yasai gyoza ramen

Serves 2    |     Prep 15 mins     |     Cook 20 mins

You can use whichever gyoza you like to make this delicious ramen your own.

Source: Wagamama Your Way

Ingredients

    /    
    /    
    /    
    /    
  • 10 steamed yasai gyoza
  • 1 pak choi
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp shichimi
  • 2 nests vegan noodles
  • 600 ml vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp veg stir-fry sauce (we like Lee Kum Kee sauce)
  • 2 tbsp wagamama vegan sambal
  • 2 spring onions, julienned
  • 1 tsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp chilli oil
